CDC Africa is the local entry point
to CDC’s global engineering platform.

Operating from Kigali, Rwanda, CDC Africa helps governments, developers, industrial operators and investment partners understand where advanced ropeway infrastructure can create measurable economic value across the continent.

The mission is simple: translate global ropeway expertise into practical African applications — from urban mobility and tourism access to mining logistics, remote connectivity and public-private infrastructure models.

African Applications

Where Ropeway Infrastructure Makes Sense.

Aerial transport can unlock value where roads are congested, terrain is complex, land acquisition is expensive or environmental impact must be minimized.

Urban Mobility

Aerial corridors for growing cities where traffic congestion, land pressure and road expansion costs limit conventional mobility options.

  • ✓ Reduce pressure on road networks
  • ✓ Connect districts with limited land use

Tourism & Eco Access

Premium, low-impact access for sensitive landscapes, conservation areas, resorts, islands and mountain destinations.

  • ✓ Enhance visitor experience
  • ✓ Minimize environmental disruption

Remote Access

Cable-based access for isolated regions, steep terrain, river crossings and locations where conventional road construction is slow or costly.

  • ✓ Connect hard-to-reach communities
  • ✓ Support regional logistics
